# The Chowk — creative brief

Hand this to any chat or tool that needs to make a poster, a post image, or a
motion video for **thechowk.online**. Everything here is checked against the
live site.

---

## The one-paragraph version

> **The Chowk** (`thechowk.online`) is an archive of 95 Indian "nostalgia
> websites" — tiny solo-built sites that each rebuild one corner of desi life
> with the music that played there: a ₹20 barbershop, a chai tapri, a truck
> cabin at 2 AM, a UP Roadways bus, a garba night, a Bengali Baul road, a
> Nusrat qawwali night. They were made by ~46 different people over three weeks
> in August 2026 and nobody had put them in one place. The Chowk shows every one
> as a live tile with its real screenshot, its maker's credit, and how many
> people are inside it right now — about **4,900 at any moment**. A chowk is the
> square where every lane meets, which is exactly what the page is.

## Brand

**Name**: द चौक / The Chowk. Hindi leads, English follows. Never "directory",
never "curated", never "archive of links".

**Mark**: a gold `TC` monogram with a wrought-iron street lantern hanging off
the C, and a chhatri (domed pavilion) silhouetted against a setting sun inside
the bowl of the C. Gold and warm amber on black, with a silver-white "TheChowk"
wordmark under it. It looks like a hand-painted signboard over a lane at dusk.

**Palette**
- `#000000` true black — the ground, always
- `#100d0a` warm near-black — anything raised off the ground
- `#e9dfcc` ivory — primary buttons and highlights
- `#b3652d` burnt amber — the live accent, the "someone is here" colour
- `#3e69a3` dusk blue — the cool counterweight, used sparingly
- `#edf0f6` off-white — text

**Type**: **Rozha One** for display (a high-contrast Devanagari-and-Latin face
that several of these sites use themselves) and **Poppins** for everything
else. Both must render Devanagari.

**Texture**: dashed graph paper, 45px cells, very low opacity, focused at the
top centre and dissolving outward. Think engineer's paper under everything, not
a spreadsheet.

## The world it lives in

Dusk in an Indian street. Lantern light, saffron and amber, dark sky. Hand-painted
shop signs, a barber's mirror, a truck's horn-OK-please tailboard, a cassette
deck, plastic chairs at a tapri, a bus window at speed. Warm, worn, specific.

**Not**: startup gradients, glassmorphic dashboards, neon cyberpunk, generic
"India" stock imagery of the Taj Mahal or spices, or anything that looks like a
SaaS landing page.

## For static posts and posters

- Black ground. Let the tiles or the artwork be the only light in the frame.
- One idea per image. The page is dense on purpose; a post about it must not be.
- Numbers are the hook: `4,900 अभी` reads better than any adjective.
- Devanagari first, Latin second and smaller, whenever both appear.
- Credit is not decoration. If a maker's site is shown, their handle goes with it.

## For motion video — the part that matters most

Every post that worked in this niche used a **screen recording with sound**.
Every static-screenshot launch in it got 0–3 likes. These sites *are* audio; a
silent frame of one is a photograph of a song.

**The shape, 20–30 seconds:**

1. **Open on ONE site actually playing, with sound from the first frame.** Not
   the grid. The viewer has to hear why this exists before they see how big it
   is. The barbershop or the truck cabin is the strongest opener.
2. **Pull back** to reveal the wall of 95 tiles.
3. **Land on the live counter ticking.** That number is the only motion in this
   whole niche that isn't a recording of somebody else's work. It is the single
   most original asset — end on it, hold it.

**Rules**: no talking head, no caption cards, no logo sting at the front, no
branded frame around the recording. It should look like a person's screen, not
an ad. Cut on the beat of whatever song is playing.

**Deliver**: 16:9 for the timeline, 9:16 for reels and status.

## Voice, in any copy that goes with it

English sentence structure carrying untranslated desi nouns: *saloon*, *tapri*,
*cutting chai*, *theka*, *Roadways*, *garba*, *chowk*, and the rupee price —
*₹20 haircut*, *₹5 ticket*. Lowercase. Short flat sentences. No em dashes.
**Zero hashtags** — not one post in this entire niche used one.

## Context worth knowing

The trend started **7 August 2026** with @s4tr2's barbershop radio, and broke
out on 8 August when @ybhrdwj shipped `saloon.wtf` — 18k likes, 5.2M views. The
Chowk collects them; it did not start them, and it must never imply otherwise.
The makers are the subject. The archive is the room they're standing in.
